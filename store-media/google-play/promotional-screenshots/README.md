# Math Brainer Promotional Screenshots

This package contains localized 1080 x 1920 Google Play screenshot creatives built from authentic Math Brainer gameplay captures for phones and tablets.

- `en/` - 19 English creatives covering the home screen and every game.
- `it/` - 19 Italian creatives covering the home screen and every game.
- `tablet-7-inch/en/` - 6 English 7-inch tablet creatives.
- `tablet-7-inch/it/` - 6 Italian 7-inch tablet creatives.
- `tablet-10-inch/en/` - 6 English 10-inch tablet creatives.
- `tablet-10-inch/it/` - 6 Italian 10-inch tablet creatives.
- `samples/` - The approved Quick Count design samples used to establish the campaign direction.
- `SELECTION.md` - Recommended eight-image primary gallery order.

The numbered filenames match across languages within each device group, making localized Play Console uploads straightforward. Original gameplay screenshots remain unchanged in `../phone-screenshots/`, `../tablet-7-inch/`, and `../tablet-10-inch/`.
