## Upload Checklist

- Set visibility to Public or Unlisted so Google Play can display the video.
- Disable age restrictions unless the final content specifically requires them.
- Verify that the video is embeddable and does not contain copyrighted material.
- Use the direct YouTube video URL in Play Console, not a playlist or channel URL.
- Confirm that no test ads, debug UI, personal notifications, or private account details are visible.
